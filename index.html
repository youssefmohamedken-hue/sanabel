About Sanabel

Technology Company.
Ecosystem Orchestrator.

Sanabel Technology and Artificial Intelligence L.L.C. is an Abu Dhabi-based AI, Digital Transformation and AgriTech company. We build intelligent platforms and digital solutions for government, semi-government and enterprise clients — and we orchestrate the UAE’s food-security ecosystem, connecting farms, technology, capital and markets. Sanabel is the prime, client-facing partner on every engagement, delivering through a carefully managed ecosystem of senior consultants, architects and technology specialists.

Rather than operating farms directly, Sanabel brings together the stakeholders required to accelerate agricultural development and create lasting impact.

The Farm Development Journey

From Assessment to Market — A Structured Path to Success

1
🔍
Step 1
Initial Assessment
Understanding the farm's current state, challenges, and development potential. Agronomic, water, and technology baseline established.
Agronomic Review Water Audit Productivity Analysis
2
📋
Step 2
Strategic Planning
A tailored development roadmap is created — defining the interventions, partners, technologies, and funding pathways most suitable for the farm.
Custom Roadmap Resource Planning Funding Strategy
3
🤝
Step 3
Partner Mobilisation
Connecting with agricultural operators, agronomists, technology providers, and funding sources. This also includes engaging buying entities for contract farming arrangements — agreements with wholesalers, retailers, food service companies, and institutional buyers to secure offtake and strengthen market linkages.
Agri Operators Contract Farming Wholesalers & Retailers Food Service
4
⚙️
Step 4
Implementation & Support
Farm development programmes delivered through qualified agricultural operators and specialist partners — with ongoing Sanabel coordination to ensure quality and alignment with the development plan.
Tech Integration Water Systems Capacity Building
5
📊
Step 5
Monitoring & Impact Measurement
Continuous tracking of farm performance, productivity outcomes, sustainability metrics, and financial results. Impact reporting aligned with GRI standards and funder requirements.
KPI Tracking GRI Reporting ESG Metrics
6
🏪
Step 6
Market Access Support
Sanabel provides active support for market access as needed — facilitating connections to buyers, distributors, food service operators, retailers, and export channels to help farms sell their produce effectively and build sustainable commercial relationships.
Buyer Connections Distribution Partners Export Channels Retail & Food Service
